    FIWARE Data Space Connector¶

    The FIWARE Data Space Connector (FIWARE DSC) is a data space connector built from the integration of open-source components of the FIWARE Dataspace Components. Any organization participating in a data space can deploy it to connect, acting as a provider of data (processing) services, a consumer of them, or both.

    The components¶

    The connector bundles several open-source frameworks:

    • Identity & Trust — OID4VC-based authentication: the VCVerifier, the Trusted Issuers List/Registry and Keycloak as credential issuer.
    • Authorization — ODRL policies enforced through APISIX (PEP), OPA (PDP) and the ODRL-PAP.
    • Catalog & Contracting — the TMForum-based product catalog and contract management.
    • Dataspace Protocol (EDC) — FDSC-EDC and the Identity Hub for DSP/DCP-based data exchange.
    • Marketplace — the Business API Ecosystem portal for publishing and buying offerings.

    See the full component overview on GitHub.
